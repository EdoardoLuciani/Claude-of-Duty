# P320 Compact — Gate 1 reference board

**Status: awaiting user approval. No modeling authorized by this board alone.**

[Approved brief](BRIEF.md) · [Primary photographic article][nra] ·
[SIG catalog, PDF page 9 / printed page 7][catalog]

## Recommendation to approve

Black **P320 Nitron Compact**, original standard **medium compact grip**, 9 mm,
3.9-inch barrel, curved trigger, no external manual safety, SIGLITE iron sights,
flush 15-round magazine. No optics cut or accessories.

**Use the early-production exterior photographed for the April 2015 American
Rifleman review as the primary appearance authority.** That review was republished
in 2020; the republishing date does not make these 2020-production photographs.
It identifies the test pistols as 9 mm with SIGLITE sights and medium grips.
This production-era choice needs explicit approval: later curved triggers,
slide catches and grip details must not be substituted simply because another
picture also says "P320 Compact".

The following images were opened and visually inspected, not selected solely
from search snippets. Some sources show more than one pistol or a different
finish; each image's allowed use is stated below. Photographs are perspective
references, **not orthographic drawings or manufacturing measurements**.

## A — Primary silhouette and controls

### A1. Compact left side — primary appearance reference

![Original Compact left-side profile, American Rifleman](https://www.americanrifleman.org/media/ut0p0mwh/sig-sauer-p320-first-review-1.jpg)

Source: [American Rifleman, "SIG Sauer P320: The First Review"][nra].

Use for the slide/frame silhouette, compact grip and baseplate outline, curved
trigger, trigger-guard shape, left-side controls, front/rear serrations, sight
placement and marking layout. The slide has substantial upper chamfers: it is
not a rectangular block with surface lines. The grip is continuously sculpted,
not an extruded slab with a separate rubber pad. Keep the front strap free of
invented finger grooves.

Do not infer material albedo by sampling this white-background photograph.
Its lighting makes the black surfaces read gray.

### A2. Compact right side — corroborating reference

![Full-size above, Compact below; right-side comparison, SWAT](https://www.swatmag.com/app/uploads/swat/2017/07/P320-2.jpg)

Source: [SWAT, "Modular Marvels"][swat]. **Only the lower pistol is Compact.**

Use the lower pistol to cross-check compact proportions, ejection-side silhouette,
right-side control placement, serial-window location, polymer panel boundaries
and short magazine baseplate. Do not copy the upper pistol's longer slide/grip.
This is a different specimen from A1; any conflicting small control detail must
be resolved in favor of A1's era, not averaged into a fictional hybrid.

### A3. Trigger, frame recesses and surface separation

![Compact trigger and right-side frame detail, American Rifleman](https://www.americanrifleman.org/media/utrfwu0l/sig-sauer-p320-first-review-3.jpg)

Same primary article. Use for the original broad curved trigger, thin guard,
rounded transitions, triangular magazine-control detail, grip relief and molded
surface texture. **No Glock-style trigger-safety blade.** Do not reproduce this
real specimen's serial number.

### C2. SIGLITE appearance in low light — behavior reference only

![Black Compact frame with localized handling marks, Lynx Defense](https://lynxdefense.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/sigp320-compact-cosmeticwear.jpg)

aggressive grip panels. Do not use this different specimen to redefine controls,

## D — Dimensions and source hierarchy

1. **Appearance:** primary 2015 Compact photo set above.
2. **Configuration and nominal scale:** [SIG 2019 catalog][catalog], PDF page 9,
   printed page 7, **COMPACT** column and lower-left Compact listing. The upper
   listings are Carry and Compact MS, not the selected pistol.
3. **Cross-checks:** SWAT for right-side appearance; ThruMyLens for early rear
   geometry/SIGLITE appearance; Lynx only for handling wear.
4. Secondary review text is not authority for unseen internal geometry, precise
   materials, dimensional tolerances or animation mechanics.

| Item | Evidence and limitation |
| --- | --- |
| Nominal overall length | SIG catalog: 183 mm / 7.2 in; use for overall visual scale |
| Nominal overall height | SIG catalog: 134 mm / 5.3 in |
| Nominal overall width | SIG catalog intends approximately 33 mm / 1.3 in; primary 2015 review lists 1.38 in. Do not assert exact width or treat overall width as slide width |
| Barrel configuration | Compact listing explicitly says 99 mm / 3.9 in; the catalog table inconsistently prints 91 mm / 3.9 in. Use the named 3.9-inch configuration, not the erroneous metric table entry |
| Magazine | SIG Compact listing: 15 rounds in 9 mm, corroborated by both primary photographic articles |
| Grip | Primary article says medium is the supplied grip; B1 visibly reads `COMPACT MEDIUM` |
| Sights | Primary article explicitly says both samples use SIGLITE three-dot night sights |
| Mass | Not needed for exterior reconstruction; catalog mass units are also inconsistent. Do not use them as precise animation physics inputs |

The catalog itself labels specifications subject to change. Published envelope
figures and uncalibrated photos do not justify a promise of submillimeter accuracy.

## Exclusions and remaining evidence limits

- **Exclude:** Full-Size, Carry, XCompact/XCarry, AXG, M17/M18, Compact MS,
  optics-ready slides, suppressor-height sights, flat triggers, aftermarket grips,
  extended magazines and later control shapes unless separately approved.
- The [2025 owner review][acheson] clearly identifies its pictured pistol as
  `320C-9-B` with **contrast sights**. It is useful for detecting the naming trap,
  not the primary SIGLITE configuration authority.
- No single calibrated six-view photographic set or 3D scan was found. The
  underside/magwell and small sight details are less well constrained than the
  side profiles. Do not invent precision: obtain further matching detail evidence
  before finalizing those areas at Gate 2, and disclose any remaining approximation.
- No live motion sequence has yet been adopted as the animation reference.
  Geometry photographs do not prove action timing. Gate 2 includes motion
  blockouts and timing approval; current game timings are not binding.
- Photography licensing is not established for redistribution. Images remain
  linked to their publishers, with attribution; no third-party image binaries
  are committed or included in the runtime asset. Create original meshes and
  textures rather than baking these photographs onto the model. Commercial
  branding clearance, if needed, is a separate human review.

## Gate 1 approval checklist

- [ ] Approve the **early-production, 2015-photo appearance**, not a mixture of
      original and later P320 controls.
- [ ] Approve A1 as primary silhouette/control authority and the limited uses of
      the supplemental images.
- [ ] Confirm black, lightly used finish; SIGLITE sights; compact medium grip;
      no accessories; 15-round flush magazine.
- [ ] Accept the documented reference limitations: permission to start geometry
      blockout is not acceptance of final fidelity or permission to invent missing
      details. Resolve remaining visible-detail evidence before Gate 2 approval.

Stop here for user approval. No `.blend`, GLB, textures or game changes have
been produced at this gate.
